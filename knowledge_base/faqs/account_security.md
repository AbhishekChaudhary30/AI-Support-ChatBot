# Customer Support FAQ: Account Access & Security

> *Notice: These support policies are sample reference data for demonstration purposes.*

## Account Access & Login Guidance

### How do I reset my password?
To reset your account password:
1. Navigate to the login page and click the **"Forgot Password?"** link.
2. Enter the email address associated with your account and click **"Send Reset Link"**.
3. Check your email inbox for a secure verification link (valid for 30 minutes).
4. Click the link and enter your new password. Ensure it has at least 10 characters, including an uppercase letter, a number, and a special character.
5. If you do not receive the email within 5 minutes, verify your spam folder or contact support.

### What should I do if my account is locked?
Accounts are automatically locked for security after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
- The lockout duration is **15 minutes**.
- You can wait 15 minutes and try again with the correct password.
- Alternatively, you can immediately initiate a password reset using the **"Forgot Password?"** link to unlock your account immediately.

### How do I set up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?
1. Log in and go to **Settings > Security & Privacy > Two-Factor Authentication**.
2. Click **"Enable 2FA"** and scan the displayed QR code with any standard TOTP authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password).
3. Enter the 6-digit confirmation code displayed on your app.
4. Save your emergency backup recovery codes in a safe place.

### How do I change my registered email address?
To change your email address:
1. Go to **Settings > Profile > Account Information**.
2. Click **"Edit"** next to your email address.
3. Enter the new email address and confirm your current password.
4. A verification link will be sent to both your old and new email addresses. You must verify both links within 24 hours to complete the change.
