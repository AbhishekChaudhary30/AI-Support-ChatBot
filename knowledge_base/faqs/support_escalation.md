# Customer Support FAQ: Support Channels, Hours & Human Escalation

> *Notice: These support policies are sample reference data for demonstration purposes.*

## Support Hours, Channels & Escalation Guidelines

### What are customer support hours and response SLAs?
- **Automated AI Support Assistant:** Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year for instant answers to knowledge base and policy questions.
- **Human Support Tier 1 & Tier 2:** Available **Monday through Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST)**, excluding US public holidays.
- **Response SLAs:**
  - Standard Community & Free users: Within 24 business hours.
  - Pro & Business Subscribers: Priority queue, response within 4 business hours.
  - Enterprise Tier: 24/7 dedicated telephone hotline and 1-hour critical incident response time.

### How can I speak to or connect with a human support agent?
If our AI assistant cannot resolve your issue, you can escalate to a human representative:
1. In the chat interface, type: *"I want to speak with a human support agent"* or click the **"Escalate to Human Agent"** button.
2. The system will compile an **Escalation Summary Card** containing your issue description and conversation context.
3. You can copy the generated summary card and submit it via our Help Desk ticket portal at `support.example.com/tickets` or email `help@support.example.com`.
4. Our support staff will receive your inquiry with all relevant context pre-filled, so you won't need to repeat yourself.

### How do I report a security vulnerability or critical incident?
For potential security issues, data privacy concerns, or critical service outages:
- Email `security@support.example.com` directly.
- Include reproduction steps and relevant logs.
- Our security response team monitors this inbox 24/7 with a 30-minute acknowledgement SLA under our Responsible Disclosure Program.
