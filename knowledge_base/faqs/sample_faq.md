# Customer Support Knowledge Base: General FAQs & Policies

## 1. Account Management & Security

### How do I reset my password?
To reset your password, visit the login page and click on "Forgot Password?". Enter your registered email address, and a secure password reset link will be sent within 2 minutes. The link expires after 30 minutes for security purposes.

### Can I enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)?
Yes. Go to **Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication**. You can use any standard TOTP authenticator app such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password.

## 2. Billing & Subscriptions

### What payment methods are accepted?
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and wire transfers for enterprise billing plans.

### What is the refund policy?
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all new monthly and annual subscriptions. If you are unsatisfied, submit a refund request through the support portal or email `billing@support.example.com` within 14 days of purchase.

### Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan at any time?
Yes. Changes to your subscription take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Upgrades will calculate prorated charges immediately.

## 3. Technical Support & System Status

### What browsers are supported?
Our web application officially supports modern versions of Google Chrome (v110+), Mozilla Firefox (v110+), Apple Safari (v16+), and Microsoft Edge (v110+).

### What should I do if the application becomes unresponsive?
1. Refresh the web page (Ctrl+F5 on Windows, Cmd+Shift+R on Mac).
2. Clear your browser cache and cookies.
3. Check the status page at `status.support.example.com` to see if there is an active incident.
4. If issues persist, contact our support team with your browser version and console error logs.

## 4. Support Hours & Escalations

### What are customer support hours?
Our automated AI support assistant is available 24/7/365. Human support agents are available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM EST. Urgent enterprise escalation tickets receive 24/7 on-call coverage.
