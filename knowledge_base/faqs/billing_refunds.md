# Customer Support FAQ: Billing, Subscriptions & Refund Policies

> *Notice: These support policies are sample reference data for demonstration purposes.*

## Refund Policy & Money-Back Guarantee

### What is the refund policy?
We offer a **14-day no-questions-asked money-back guarantee** for all new subscription plans (both monthly and annual).
- If you are not satisfied with the service within the first 14 days of your initial purchase, you are entitled to a full 100% refund.
- To request a refund, submit a request through the customer support portal or email `billing@support.example.com` with your invoice number.
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method within **3 to 5 business days** depending on your bank.
- Renewal charges after the initial 14-day window are non-refundable, but you can cancel future renewals at any time.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your subscription at any time without fees:
1. Navigate to **Billing & Plans > Manage Subscription**.
2. Click **"Cancel Subscription"** at the bottom of the page.
3. Complete the confirmation step.
4. Your account will remain active with full access until the end of your current paid billing period, and no further charges will occur.

### Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
- **Upgrades:** When upgrading to a higher tier, changes take effect immediately. You will be billed a prorated charge for the remaining days of your current billing cycle.
- **Downgrades:** When downgrading, the change takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle. No immediate refund is issued for the difference.

### What payment methods are accepted?
We accept:
- All major credit and debit cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover.
- Digital wallets: Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal.
- Enterprise wire transfers / ACH for annual enterprise contracts over $2,000.
