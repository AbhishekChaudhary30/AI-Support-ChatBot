# Customer Support FAQ: Orders, Deliveries & Shipping

> *Notice: These support policies are sample reference data for demonstration purposes.*

## Order Processing & Shipping Guidelines

### What are standard delivery times and shipping costs?
- **Standard Ground Shipping:** 3 to 5 business days. Free for all orders over $50, or a flat $4.99 fee for orders under $50.
- **Express Shipping:** 1 to 2 business days. Available for $14.99 at checkout.
- **International Shipping:** 7 to 14 business days depending on customs clearance and destination country. Customs duties and import taxes are the responsibility of the recipient.

### How do I track my order shipment?
Once your order ships, an automated shipping confirmation email is dispatched containing your courier tracking number (USPS, FedEx, or DHL).
- You can track packages directly on the carrier website using the provided tracking code.
- You can also view active tracking updates in **Account > Order History > Track Package**.

### What should I do if my order is delayed or missing?
If your order has not arrived within **2 business days past the estimated delivery window**:
1. Check the tracking number for carrier delay notices or weather exceptions.
2. Confirm that the shipping address on your order receipt is accurate.
3. Check with household members or building reception.
4. Contact customer support with your Order ID. If the shipment is verified lost by the carrier, we will issue an immediate replacement or full refund.

### Can I cancel or change an order after placing it?
Orders can be modified or cancelled within **1 hour of submission** directly from your **Order History** page. After 1 hour, orders are routed to our fulfillment warehouse for packaging and cannot be cancelled prior to shipment; however, you may initiate a return upon receipt under our 14-day refund policy.
